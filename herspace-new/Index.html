Herspace: An Overview

About Us

HerSpace is a grassroots, youth-led, women-led organization working at the intersection of gender equality and climate action. Through leadership development, community building, and intersectional advocacy, we aim to prevent gender-based violence, strengthen SRHR, and embed feminist and indigenous approaches into climate solutions.

Our work is grounded in decolonial feminist values and aligns with the Sustainable Development Goals:

Our Thematic Areas

Feminist Climate Justice

Addressing gendered impacts of climate change

Sexuality, Gender, Health, and Rights (SRHR)

Advancing SRHR & tackling taboos

Youth-led Feminist Movement Building

Grassroots advocacy

Our Mission

HerSpace powers an interconnected movement of young leaders across Pakistan to advance gender justice and climate justice. We create the space, support, and community young people need to transform their ideas into action. This purpose drives everything we do.

Our Belief

Real and lasting change starts with youth. When young people, especially girls and women, have the space, resources, and solidarity to act, they can shift mindsets, mobilize communities, and transform entire systems.

Our Supporters

Our Approach

HerSpace operates as a platform by and for youth, focusing on leadership, learning, and collective action. We connect grassroots energy with national and global momentum. When young people, especially girls and marginalized genders, are trusted with space, tools, and solidarity, they can turn ideas into movements and movements into systemic change.
